# Fraud Review Policy

## Analyst review

High-risk model outputs must be reviewed by an analyst. The model is a prioritisation aid, not an
automated decision system. A score decides the order in which an analyst looks at transactions;
it never closes a case, blocks an account or refunds a payment on its own.

## Uncertain scores

A score close to the threshold is uncertain. The analyst needs more evidence before acting:
the customer's recent history, the destination account, and whether the balance was drained.

## What the assistant must not do

The assistant must not provide financial, credit, legal, tax or investment advice. It must not
approve or decline a loan, waive a fraud flag, close an account or reverse a transfer. Requests
like these are refused before any document is searched.
