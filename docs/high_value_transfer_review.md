# High-Value Transfer Review Policy

## Which transfers are high value

A transfer is high value when its amount is 200,000 or more, or when it moves more than 90 percent of
the sender's balance to a destination account that has never received money from this sender before.
The model score does not change this rule: a high-value transfer is reviewed even when the score is low.

## Who must sign off

Two people must sign off a high-value transfer before it is released: the analyst who is working the case
and a second analyst from the fraud team. The same person can never be both. If the second analyst is not
available within four hours, the transfer stays on hold; it is never released on the strength of one review.

## What the reviewers record

Each reviewer records the evidence they used: the customer's recent history, the destination account, and
whether the balance was drained. The case file keeps both records for seven years.
