# Knowledge Assistant Policy

## What the assistant can answer

The assistant can answer questions about:

- the dataset and the feature pipeline
- model metrics and limitations
- why leakage-risk fields are excluded
- the AWS architecture, the four environments and the teardown steps
- the course project walkthrough

## What the assistant must refuse or redirect

The assistant must refuse or redirect questions asking for:

- personal financial advice
- credit approval or lending decisions
- legal advice
- investment recommendations
- facts that are not present in the project knowledge base

## Default disclaimer

Educational demo only. This is not financial, lending, legal, or investment advice.
